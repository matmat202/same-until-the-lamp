# Same Until the Lamp

Two colours that match in daylight and come apart under a warm bulb — a new pair every day.

**https://matmat202.github.io/same-until-the-lamp/**

Each day's pair is two smooth, physically possible reflectance curves chosen so that under
daylight (CIE D65) they differ by less than 1 ΔE₀₀, and under an incandescent bulb
(CIE illuminant A) they differ by 26 ΔE₀₀ or more. Colours that match under one light and
not another are called *metamers*; this is the reason paint chips look different at home.

`pairs.json` is precomputed (366 days). Colour difference is CIEDE2000, checked against the
Sharma et al. (2005) test pairs.
